## Additional Sibling Repositories

Alongside `staff-app/`, `membership-management/`, and `auth-server/`, this umbrella workspace also contains:

- `management-infra/`: Local Docker Compose stack (`compose.yaml`, Postgres 18 with `auth` and `members` databases, migration & seed services) mirroring the AWS deployment, plus `DEPLOYMENT-ROADMAP.md`. See `management-infra/README.md`.
- `ekklesiaio-landing/`: Public bilingual (`en`/`es`) landing page and waitlist (`https://ekklesiaio.com`). Consumes brand tokens generated by `pnpm tokens` in `staff-app/`. See `ekklesiaio-landing/CLAUDE.md`.
